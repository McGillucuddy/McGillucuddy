# Asset credits

All 3D models and photo-scanned materials in this folder come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under **CC0** (public domain). No attribution is required, but thank you to the Poly Haven team and its artists.

Each model is the 1K glTF version (`models/<name>/<name>_1k.gltf` plus its `.bin` and `textures/`). Each material is the 1K JPG set: colour (`diff`), OpenGL normal (`nor_gl`) and roughness (`rough`).

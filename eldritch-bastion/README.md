# Eldritch Bastion

A Lovecraftian tower defense game. The stars are right, Cthulhu and the elder gods have awoken, and you have to hold the city.

**To play:** open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing to install. Progress is saved in your browser.

## Features
- **Open-field siege:** there are no roads. Horrors emerge from the darkness at the map edges and swarm across the field toward your walled city, flowing around your towers. Build to funnel them, but you can never seal the city off. Horrors that reach the walls claw at them, draining sanity until you kill them.
- **5 maps:** Innsmouth Harbor, Miskatonic University, Dunwich Hills, Arkham and R'lyeh Ascendant. Each has its own boss. Later maps are attacked from more directions.
- **Night lighting:** only the city and your towers give light. Out in the dark you see the horrors' eyes before you see them. Windows go dark and fires break out as sanity falls, ichor stains stay on the field, and a red mist marks where the next wave will come from.
- **7 towers, each with 4 upgrade tiers:** Militia Marksman, Dynamite Mortar, Elder Sign Ward (slows and reveals), Tesla Coil (chain lightning), Occult Scholar (bolts that ignore armor), Lighthouse Beam (a beam that ramps up) and Curio Shop (income).
- **16 enemies** with their own mechanics: flyers that ignore the road, heavy armor, phasing, blinking, Shoggoths that split, madness auras that slow nearby towers, and summoners.
- **Bosses:** Father Dagon, The Haunter of the Dark, The Dunwich Horror, Nyarlathotep and Great Cthulhu.
- **3 spells:** Elder Sign, Flame of Cthugha and Powder of Ibn-Ghazi.
- **Progression:** clearing a map unlocks the next map, new towers and Nightmare mode. Elder Signs (★, up to 3 per map per difficulty) pay for a research tree of 10 upgrades.
- **Hand-drawn-in-code models:** every monster and tower is drawn procedurally with jointed limbs, shading and animation, rendered at 2× resolution (it drops to 1× automatically on slow machines).
- A bestiary, targeting modes, calling waves early for bonus gold, 1×/2×/3× speed, and sound effects synthesized in the browser.

## Controls
`1`–`7` pick a tower · click to place (`Shift` places several) · `U` upgrade · `S` sell · `Space` calls the next wave (calling early pays bonus gold) · `Q`/`W`/`E` cast spells · `F` changes speed · `P` pauses · `Esc` cancels

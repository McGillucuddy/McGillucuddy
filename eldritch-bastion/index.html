Eldritch Bastion

The stars are right. The Old Ones have awoken.
Hold the city until dawn… or until madness takes you.

How to Play

Build towers anywhere on the open field. Horrors emerge from the darkness at the edges and flow around your towers toward the city, so you can funnel them — but you may never seal the city off completely. Select one from the Defenses panel (or press 1–7), then click an empty tile. Hold Shift to place several.

Call waves with the button or Space. Calling early while horrors are still on the field grants bonus gold.

Click a tower to upgrade it (U), sell it (S) or change who it targets.

Spells: Q Elder Sign stuns everything, W Flame of Cthugha burns an area, E Powder of Ibn-Ghazi (researched) reveals and weakens.

City Sanity drains every second that horrors claw at the walls. Kill them before the city loses its mind. A red mist marks where the next wave will come from.

  • Flyers pass over everything and head straight for the walls. Mortars can't hit them.
  • Armored foes shrug off weak shots. Arcane bolts and beams pierce armor.
  • Phasing horrors can only be hit while revealed by a Ward or Scholar.
  • Madness auras halve the fire rate of nearby towers.

Progression: clearing a map unlocks the next map and new towers. Earn up to 3 Elder Signs ★ per map per difficulty, then spend them on Forbidden Research. Clear a map to unlock its Nightmare mode.

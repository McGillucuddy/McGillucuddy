# Death Row Derby: lore

This is the canon so far. Everything here is shown in the game somewhere (mostly in the garage graffiti, `js/graffiti.js`),
so treat it as fixed, and add to it here first when something new is introduced. Mark open questions as open.

## The world

- **State Penitentiary 9**, known inside as **Cellblock 9** or **C9**, runs the **Death Row Derby**: armed races on
  public roads, broadcast to the city. Prisoners drive and shoot; the crowd and the sponsors pay.
- The Derby is in its **41st season** (Derby XLI). Inmates date their dead by Derby season, in Roman numerals.
- The prize is **freedom**. Win the Crown and you walk free. Whether anyone ever has is the most argued-about
  question on the walls.
- The race climbs the city in four acts, from the bottom to the top:
  **the Undercity** → **the Stacks** → **the Gilded Terraces** → **the Crown**.
- **The cameras are always on.** Every garage has CCTV; the inmates spray stencils of crossed-out cameras.

## People and powers

- **Warden Halloran** runs C9 and the Derby. The inmates call him a liar and a coward ("HALLORAN LIES",
  "WARDEN HALLORAN: COWARD").
- **Aureum** is the corporation behind the Derby's top tier: the **Aureum Grand Prix** at the Crown, and the money that
  runs the Gilded Terraces. "AUREUM OWNS YOU."
- **Inmate 4471** is the player (the default inmate number). Someone has written "4471 IS NEXT" on the wall.

## The bosses

- **The Turnkey** (Act I, the Undercity): the warden's enforcer, in an armoured prison van. "TURNKEY = HALLORAN'S DOG",
  "THE TURNKEY HAS A KEY FOR EVERY CELL".
- **Smokestack Sal** (Act II, the Stacks): king of the foundry district, mines and smog. The inmates blame Sal's smoke
  for what happened to **D Block** ("SAL'S SMOKE TOOK D BLOCK"). The writer **Rusk** died in Derby XL and "was right
  about Sal" (*open: what Rusk knew*).
- **Augustin Vale** (Act III, the Gilded Terraces): the sponsors' golden boy in a gold supercar. The walls say his
  father bought his seat ("VALE'S DADDY BOUGHT HIS SEAT"; *open: is his father Aureum's chairman?*).
- **Vex "The Apex" Marlowe** (Act IV, the Crown): the undefeated champion. Marlowe **was a C9 inmate**, number 0001,
  the first ("MARLOWE WAS ONE OF US", "C9 0001 = MARLOWE"). The walls say Marlowe won and **took the deal**, staying
  on as champion instead of walking free, and some call that betrayal ("MARLOWE TOOK THE DEAL" / "TRAITOR").
  *Open: what the deal was.*

## Inmates and legends

- **Dez**: the one inmate said to have **walked free**. The walls argue about it in two hands:
  "DEZ WALKED FREE" / "NO HE DIDN'T", and "NOBODY WALKS FREE" / "DEZ DID". *Open: did Dez walk, and where is Dez now?*
- **The dead**, on the memorial wall: Tommy K (Derby XXXVIII), Old Man Rey (XXXIX), Bones, Rusk and Little Jo (XL),
  Santi (XLI, this season).
- "They stopped counting at 300": the Derby's death toll, as the inmates tell it.
- Crews and writers on the walls: **C9K** (Cellblock 9 Kings), **D Block**, **Lifers**, **Yard Boyz**, **WKND**;
  writers Kase, Rusk, Doze, Nomad, Slab, Omen, Grim, Solo, Mako, Zero, Havoc, Bones, Reks, Toxe and Dez.
- Sayings: "LAP 3 IS WHERE THEY GET YOU", "C9 NEVER FORGETS", "FREE C9", "NO GODS NO WARDENS", "GOLD BLEEDS TOO",
  "THE CROWN EATS ITS OWN", "BREATHE DEEP KID" (Sal's line), "SMILE FOR THE SPONSORS".

## About this game

**An inmate drives. You shoot. Win, and you both walk free.**

*Death Row Derby* is a first-person combat-racing roguelite. You don't drive: you're the gunner, riding shotgun with a pistol, a blast hatch and a handful of grenades while a fellow prisoner keeps the car on the road. Every race is broadcast live to the city above. Finish in the top three to stay in the run. Miss the cut three times and you go back to your cell.

**Every run is a new season**
Pick your route each night from a wall of TV channels: races, elite races, bounty hunts, shopping nights, contraband stashes and back-room deals. Four acts climb from the Undercity to the Crown at the top of the Spire. Each act ends against a boss, and only an outright win against the champion sets you free.

**Fight from the passenger seat**
- Thirteen guns, from the service pistol to a drum-fed Typewriter, a pump shotgun, a harpoon gun, a flamethrower and rocket launchers. Mount a belt-fed gun on the roof and climb up to it.
- Slam the blast hatch to stop bullets, and time it on an incoming rocket to bat it back.
- Lob grenades through a rival's open roof hatch, or shoot their driver through the glass.
- Lean out of the window, kick out the windscreen, or take a moment of **Focus**: slow motion when it matters.

**Your driver is a person, not a steering wheel**
Recruit inmates with names and histories. They dodge rockets and gunfire on their own, and they get better at it the longer they survive with you. Shout orders: ram that car, get alongside, floor it. Shoot them by accident and they'll remember it.

**Rivals with grudges, bosses with tricks**
The field fights back, and it fights itself: rivals settle scores with whoever hit them last. Wreck someone and they may come back for revenge later in the run. Each boss drives their own machine:
- **The Turnkey's** prison bus is packed with armed lifers.
- **Smokestack Sal** hooks you with a tow truck and drags you backwards.
- **Augustin Vale** brings a gold security detail.
- **Vex "The Apex" Marlowe's** hearse won't stay down.

Beat them to win their rides for yourself.

**Build it, paint it, make it yours**
Engines, armour, tyres and nitro, tuned and fitted between races. Bull bars, roof cages, liveries and rims. Kill marks are stencilled on your car, and boss trophies are bolted onto the bodywork. Customize your own character too.

**Never the same race twice**
Six kinds of track, from city blocks and hairpin switchbacks to motorways and drag strips, generated fresh every race. There's weather, from fog to ion storms, and a darksynth soundtrack composed live for every race.

## Key features (short list for the sidebar or a trailer card)

- First-person gunner: you shoot, an AI inmate drives.
- Roguelite runs: four acts, a route you choose, three strikes.
- 13 weapons, roof-mounted guns, grenades and a blast hatch.
- Four bosses with their own gimmicks, and their cars to unlock.
- Drivers who learn and remember; rivals who hold grudges.
- Six procedurally generated track types, plus dynamic weather.
- Deep car and character customization.
- An original synthesized darksynth soundtrack.

## itch.io page (shorter)

**Death Row Derby.** Ride shotgun in a prison death race.

An inmate drives, you shoot. Fight through four acts of armed rivals and armoured bosses on live TV, choose your route each night, upgrade and paint your car, and win the Crown to walk free. Three strikes and you're back in your cell.

Play in your browser, or download it for Windows, Linux or macOS.

**Controls:**
- Mouse: aim. Left mouse button: fire. Right mouse button: aim down the sights.
- R: reload. G: grenade. Space (hold): blast hatch.
- Shift: Focus (slow motion). V: roof gun. T: emotes. C: orders for your driver. Esc: pause.

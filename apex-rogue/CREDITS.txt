DEATH ROW DERBY
Category 2 Games

Design, code, models, sound effects and music were made for the game. Every car, gun, person and track
is built in code; every gunshot, engine and song is synthesised as you play.

VOICES
Spoken lines synthesised with Piper (MIT licence) from the LibriTTS voice model, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). LibriTTS: H. Zen, V. Dang, R. Clark, Y. Zhang, R. J. Weiss,
Y. Jia, Z. Chen and Y. Wu, "LibriTTS: A Corpus Derived from LibriSpeech for Text-to-Speech" (2019), derived
from LibriSpeech and LibriVox recordings. The lines were processed (pitched, filtered, distorted) for the game.

How to Play

The map. Each floor is a branching map. Pick your path: Combat, Elite (harder, drops a relic), Unknown events, Rest sites, the Antiquarian's shop, and Treasure. The boss waits at the top.

Your turn. You get 3 energy and draw 5 cards. Click a card to play it. If it needs a target, click a horror or an ally. Unspent cards are discarded when you click End Turn.

Intents. Every horror shows what it will do next. A sword with a number means an attack, and hovering a horror draws a line to whoever it is aiming at. Each hero shows the total damage coming their way, after their Block.

Your companions act after you, on their own. Their intent is shown over their heads: the Warden taunts horrors onto himself, the Physician heals and revives, and the Hunter and Occultist strike. Plan around them.

Ranks. Your party stands in four ranks: Warden, Hunter, Occultist, Physician. Melee horrors can only reach the front two. Cards marked front can only hit the two nearest horrors.

Block absorbs damage and fades at the start of its owner's turn. Taunt forces single-target attacks onto the taunter. Vulnerable takes 50% more, Weak deals 25% less, and Bleed hurts every turn.

If you fall, the expedition ends. Fallen companions can be revived by the Physician, at rest sites, or with salts.

Choose Your Calling

The other three callings join you as companions. They act on their own: the Warden holds attention, the Physician keeps everyone alive.

Forbidden Rites

Spend Forbidden Knowledge earned on expeditions. Rites are permanent. Refund any time.

How to Play

Move with WASD. Aim with the mouse and hold left click to attack.

Abilities: 1 2 3. Dodge roll: Space, which makes you briefly untouchable. Healing Draught: F.

Interact / revive: hold E next to a fallen ally, or press it at altars, chests, fires and the merchant.

The party. Horrors attack whoever has drawn the most threat. The Warden generates lots of it and taunts horrors off the others. Hit too hard, too early, and they'll come for you instead. Healing draws a little attention too.

Red ground is about to hurt. Watch for circles, cones and lines, and get out before they fill. Bosses cast with a bar you can read at the top of the screen.

The run. Clear a room, then pick the next door: Combat, Elite (relic), Shrine (boon), Rest, Merchant or Treasure. Each floor ends in a boss. Level-ups let you pick boons. If the whole party falls, the expedition is over, but you keep the Forbidden Knowledge you earned.

On a phone: drag on the left side to move. You'll aim and attack the nearest horror automatically. Tap the buttons for abilities.
